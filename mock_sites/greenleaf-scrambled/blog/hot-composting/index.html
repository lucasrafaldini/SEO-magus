Hot Composting Method

Hot composting needs a pile of at least one cubic metre, a carbon to nitrogen ratio near 30 to 1 and turning every few days.

New to this? Read the complete composting guide before building your first pile.

A compost tumbler keeps the heat in and makes turning much easier.
