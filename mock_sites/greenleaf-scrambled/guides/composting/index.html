Complete Composting Guide

Composting turns kitchen scraps and garden waste into rich humus. Balance green and brown materials, keep the pile moist and turn it regularly.

If you want finished compost in weeks rather than months, try hot composting: a large, well-aerated pile heats up and breaks down fast.

A smelly or slow pile usually has a simple cause; our compost bin troubleshooting checklist covers the common fixes, click here.

Turning a heavy pile by hand is hard work, which is why many gardeners switch to a compost tumbler.

Finished compost is the best amendment for raised bed soil in the vegetable patch.
